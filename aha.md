## Canonical visual grammar

Each Aha should stand on its own:

```text
BEFORE — a complete mock game screen
        ↓
ACTION — the player does one meaningful thing
        ↓
AFTER — a complete mock game screen with a clearly changed system
        ↓
AHA — one sentence explaining the mental-model shift
```

The visual change is the primary explanation. Text is annotation only.
